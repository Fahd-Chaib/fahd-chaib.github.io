How It Earns Uploader

Private publishing tool for the How It Earns YouTube channel.

What this app does

How It Earns Uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the How It Earns YouTube channel. It uploads the channel's own short videos, with their titles and descriptions, to that single YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public and no other users can sign in.

Privacy policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026.

  • Data accessed: the app requests only the YouTube permission needed to upload videos (youtube.upload) to the owner's channel.
  • Data use: this permission is used solely to upload videos produced for the How It Earns channel. The app does not read, collect, sell or share any personal data, viewer data or channel analytics.
  • Storage: the authorization token is stored privately by the channel owner and used only to perform uploads.
  • Third parties: no data is shared with third parties. Use of YouTube is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Revoking access: access can be revoked at any time from Google Account permissions.
  • Contact: via the How It Earns YouTube channel.
